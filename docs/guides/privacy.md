# Local data, privacy, and ownership

Hatrack is your local organiser, not another account or a cloud service.

## What stays on your computer

Profile metadata, icons, and new app-state directories are stored under your local Hatrack folder. No Hatrack telemetry or credential upload is implemented. Vendor apps and your browser retain their own networking and privacy behaviour.

## Removal keeps account data

Removing a launcher deletes its library entry and owned shortcuts, while retaining profile folders. Uninstalling Hatrack preserves the catalogue and profile data. Reinstall and save a profile to recreate its shortcuts.

## Ownership and MIT

Hatrack is a personal project by Muneef Mumthas. Original software and artwork use the MIT licence, which permits modification and commercial redistribution with the notice retained. Dependencies, vendor app logos, and product names retain their own rights. The website saves only your appearance preference in local browser storage.

## The boundary of isolation

Separate local state is not an operating-system sandbox. Profiles run as the same Windows user and external integrations can share state. Use separate Windows users if you need a stronger security boundary.
