# Custom colours and icons for desktop profiles

Recolour the Claude or Codex app logo, or upload your own image.

## Choose a built-in design

Choose the matching Claude or Codex desktop logo. Pick a preset, use the RGB colour picker, or enter a hex value. Recolouring preserves the original logo silhouette. The Hatrack logo remains separate.

## Upload your own image

PNG, JPEG, and ICO images are decoded locally. Use images at most 10 MB and 4096 pixels per side. Adjust image size, padding, horizontal and vertical crop, and background while checking the previews.

## Desktop and taskbar consistency

Hatrack generates multiple icon sizes and uses the same artwork across its shortcuts and profile windows. Reopen a running profile after editing its icon. Windows may require unpinning and repinning an old taskbar shortcut.
